Vibrant directions for the PRD site

Same kolam idea (places are dots, a plan is the line through them), now in colour. What changes from the first version:

  • Colour does the work. Big colour fields per section, not a grey document with one accent.
  • Less like a document. No hairline rows everywhere. Stages become coloured tiles you can scan; reading text sits on calm panels between them.
  • One idea per screen. Every section has one headline, one picture or film, and a few lines of text.
